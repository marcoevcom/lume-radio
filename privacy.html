Privacy Policy – Lume Radio Uploader

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Lume Radio Uploader is a private, non-commercial tool operated by the owner of the Lume Radio YouTube channel (youtube.com/@lume-radio). It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.

What it does

The tool uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and thumbnails to the Lume Radio channel and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, translations, playlists and privacy settings.

Data accessed

  • Access to the channel owner's own YouTube account, granted through Google's OAuth sign-in, limited to managing that account's videos.
  • No data from any other person or channel is accessed, collected or stored.

Storage and sharing

  • The access token is stored only on the channel owner's computer and is never shared or sold.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is performed by this tool.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and through the YouTube API Services: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
